# Personalised first-visit setup

The Placement Desk now opens a lightweight first-visit setup that asks about region, preferred pharmacy environment, top priorities, salary/hours constraints and community-pharmacy comfort.

The answers feed the existing recommendation weights and filters rather than acting as decorative profile data. Users can retake the setup from Preferences or the intro panel, and the final choices remain editable through the normal interface.

The setup is designed to reduce information overload: one question at a time, clear choices, visible progress and repeated reassurance that nothing is permanent.

The footer also carries a small handwritten-style `made by Phivos` signature while preserving the original open-source attribution and product limitations.
