Privacy Policy

Your choices should remain yours. This notice explains what Placement Atlas processes when you browse programmes, sign in with Google or email, save a shortlist, or use map and diagnostic features.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Placement Atlas (placementatlas.co.uk) is an independent web application created by Phivos Michailides to help prospective foundation pharmacists explore and compare training programmes in England and Wales. It is not operated by Oriel, NHS England, a training provider or the General Pharmaceutical Council.

For questions about this notice, your personal data, or a request to access or delete an account and its saved information, contact phivmich1@gmail.com.

2. Information you provide and information we process

Browsing without an account

You can explore Placement Atlas without signing in. The app stores choices in your browser, including shortlist decisions, rankings, notes, comparison selections, quiz answers, filters, display settings and optional commute starting points. Uploaded programme CSV files and locally prepared exports may also be saved in browser storage. Browser data normally remains on that device unless you clear it, export it or choose to sync it to an account.

Optional Google or email sign-in

If you choose to create an account, our authentication provider, Supabase, processes account identifiers and sign-in information. Email sign-in uses the email address you enter. Google sign-in may provide your Google account's basic identity information, including an account identifier, email address, name and profile picture if made available by Google. We use that information to authenticate you and display your signed-in account, not to access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or calendars.

Optional member profile

After sign-in, you can complete a member profile with your first and last name, chosen username, date of birth, university and study year. The account setup currently asks for these fields, although you can close it and continue using the placement tools. Your username is reserved uniquely and is designed to be visible to signed-in members for future member-discovery features. Your full name, date of birth, university and study year are stored separately in account-restricted database records and are not published to the member directory. Your display name and profile photo may be shown in your own account area; broader sharing features are not yet enabled. You can edit your details in Profile or contact us to request deletion.

Saved account workspace

When you sign in and choose to save or import a browser workspace, the app syncs saved choices to a private Supabase database record associated with your account. This may contain your preferred locations, postcode or commute starting point, shortlist and exclusion decisions, notes, quiz/profile preferences, saved programme data, Oriel simulation selections and appearance settings. Your optional commute postcode can be included in the cloud workspace.

Technical information

When you visit the site, hosting and integrated services can process ordinary connection data such as IP address, browser type and requested pages. We use Sentry to diagnose JavaScript errors. Error reports can include event times, page addresses, browser and device information, technical stack traces and associated diagnostic context. Do not enter sensitive personal information into free-text notes.

3. Why we use this information

  • To provide programme exploration, filtering, comparisons and saved personal preferences.
  • To authenticate optional accounts, maintain member profiles, reserve unique usernames and save or restore workspaces across devices.
  • To provide requested map, postcode and travel-time features.
  • To investigate bugs, protect the service and improve reliability.
  • To answer your support questions and honour data-related requests.

We do not sell your personal information or use Google sign-in information to serve targeted advertising.

4. Google sign-in: specific disclosure

When you select Continue with Google, Google authenticates your identity through Google Identity Services or a supported OAuth flow managed by Supabase. Placement Atlas uses the resulting identity information only to create or recognise your account, show your signed-in identity and associate your private saved workspace with that account.

Google account identity information is processed by Google and Supabase as part of authentication, and may be stored with your Supabase account record. We do not request permission to read Gmail messages, files or contacts, do not publish content to your Google account, and do not sell or transfer Google identity data to advertisers.

Signing in is optional. You can use the programme explorer without a Google account and can request deletion of the Placement Atlas account information associated with Google sign-in using the contact address above. You can also manage third-party access in your Google Account; disconnecting Google is separate from deleting the data stored by Placement Atlas.

5. Services that may receive data

We use external providers to operate particular parts of the app. The information each receives depends on which features you use:

  • Supabase — account authentication and optional private cloud-workspace storage.
  • Google — identity authentication, only when you select Google sign-in.
  • Sentry — application error reports and technical diagnostics.
  • Map and location providers (including OpenStreetMap-based tile providers and MapTiler where enabled) — map assets and necessary connection or map-view requests.
  • Postcodes.io — postcode lookups when you choose a postcode search.
  • Transport for London (TfL) — relevant starting and destination locations when you request travel estimates.
  • GitHub Pages and content-delivery providers — delivery of the public website and its assets.

These services may independently process connection details and operate under their own privacy notices. Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom; applicable safeguards and provider arrangements govern those transfers.

6. Browser storage and cookies

The site uses browser storage to remember programme choices, preferences, imported data, display settings and, for signed-in visitors, authentication sessions. Some files may be cached to allow offline access. These functions are necessary to keep your workspace working. Third-party services may use their own storage mechanisms when you access their functionality. You can clear locally stored information through browser settings or supported reset/export controls, although doing so can remove unsynced decisions.

7. Legal bases and your choices

Where UK data protection law applies, we use information as necessary to provide the service or account features you request, and on legitimate interests in maintaining security, diagnosing errors and improving reliability, balanced against your rights. Features such as Google sign-in, cloud saves and entering a commute location are optional. You can decline them and still browse the programme catalogue.

8. Retention, security and deletion

Local browser data remains until you or your browser remove it, subject to browser behaviour. Saved account data remains associated with your account unless deleted following a request or service change. Operational logs and diagnostic records are retained according to the relevant provider settings and retention arrangements; backups and necessary security records may take additional time to expire.

We use HTTPS and Supabase authentication and database access controls to help protect saved accounts. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If you want to delete your Placement Atlas account and its cloud workspace, email phivmich1@gmail.com from your account email address (or explain how we can verify ownership). We will assess and process the request in accordance with applicable law.

9. Your data protection rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or to object to certain processing. You may also complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office. Contact us first if you would like help exercising a right or understanding which information is stored.

10. Research features, external links and policy changes

A previous optional in-app usability study is paused. Existing study results may remain in browser storage until deleted or exported by the participant; signed-in workspace sync may include previously saved local study data. The app contains links to independent websites with separate privacy policies.

This notice may change as features or providers change. We will update its date and, where required, provide further notice before making materially different uses of personal data.

Need help? Contact phivmich1@gmail.com with privacy, account or deletion questions.
