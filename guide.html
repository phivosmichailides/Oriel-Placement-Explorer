How to compare and shortlist Oriel foundation pharmacist training programmes

Choosing a foundation training programme is about more than a hospital name. Training settings, travel between sites, pay, hours and personal priorities can all affect which options suit you.

Placement Atlas is an independent web app built to help applicants explore and compare the programme catalogue, organise decisions and understand trade-offs before using the official Oriel system.

What matters when comparing pharmacy foundation training placements?

1. Training setting and rotations

Look at whether a programme is hospital-based, general practice, community pharmacy, industry or split between settings. A multi-site programme may offer a different type of experience from a single-site role. Read each programme description, and verify the actual structure with the employer.

2. Location and journeys

The training address on a listing may not represent every site where you will work. Check the locations of rotations, commuting options and how realistic the travel would be for your circumstances. Map markers and distances are helpful starting points, but they are not guarantees of journey times.

3. Salary and working hours

Compare the advertised salary and contracted weekly hours together, rather than relying only on annual pay. Listings can be incomplete or change; verify current terms and whether there are differences across rotation sites.

4. Your priorities and uncertainties

Record your preferences and questions as you explore. A programme that suits one applicant may not suit another. Pay, clinical exposure, development opportunities, location and personal circumstances deserve different weights for different people.

How Placement Atlas helps you organise your shortlist

  1. Explore: Browse the England and Wales catalogue and filter programmes by relevant fields such as region, setting, salary and hours.
  2. Map: View mapped locations and investigate sites, distance and transport context where the data is available.
  3. Personalise: Adjust the importance of different factors and inspect the reasons and trade-offs behind suggested fit scores. These scores are heuristics, not workplace reviews or official recommendations.
  4. Compare: Keep programmes side by side so that pay, location, description and training settings are easier to evaluate.
  5. Shortlist: Record decisions, notes and priorities, reorder candidates and export or back up the results. Oriel preferences are not changed by this tool.

The application also includes an illustrative outcome simulator. It explores hypothetical ranking scenarios and does not predict or reproduce official allocation decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Is Placement Atlas the official Oriel website?

No. It is an independent decision-support application. Official applications, offers, rankings, deadlines and programme changes must be handled and checked through the official recruitment services.

Does the tool list every UK foundation pharmacist training programme?

No. Its built-in October 2026 snapshot covers England and Wales only. It is not a comprehensive database for every UK nation or future recruitment round.

Are the programmes and salaries updated live?

No. The embedded catalogue is based on a dated export. Information may change, and the site provides an option to upload a more recent programme CSV for your own use. Always check official programme information.

Can I use Placement Atlas to submit my Oriel preferences?

No. You can explore, compare, record and export your decisions, but submitting or changing preferences happens only through Oriel.

Is the personal fit score an objective quality rating?

No. It reflects programme data and user-selected priorities. It cannot confirm the day-to-day workload, working conditions, supervision quality or eligibility for a position.

Can I save my shortlist?

Yes. The app supports local saving and backups, with optional account-based synchronisation for supported data. Export your decisions and keep a backup before switching devices or clearing browser storage.
